Natural Beauty

Redwood & Wood Fencing

Redwood is the premium choice for wood fencing in Hawaii. It's naturally resistant to rot and insects, strong, durable, and adds warmth and beauty to any property. Wood fencing provides unmatched security and privacy.

We build custom wood fences and gates including privacy fencing, sliding gates, and swing gates using quality redwood and cedar lumber selected for Hawaii's conditions.

Zero Maintenance

Trex Seclusions Composite Fencing

Trex Seclusions is the ultimate low-maintenance privacy fence. Made from a blend of recycled wood and plastic, it won't rot, warp, crack, or splinter. No painting or staining required -- ever.

The Trex system delivers the natural look of wood with the durability of composite materials. It's engineered to stand up to Hawaii's sun, rain, and salt air without fading or deteriorating.

Premium Vinyl Wood-Grain

Bufftech CertaGrain

Want the look of wood without any of the maintenance? Bufftech CertaGrain vinyl fencing features a realistic wood-grain texture that rivals the appearance of natural wood.

Available in Sierra Blend and other earth-tone colors, CertaGrain is backed by a lifetime limited warranty and never needs painting, staining, or sealing.

Wood & Composite Questions

Redwood and cedar are excellent choices for Hawaii due to their natural resistance to rot and insects. They do require periodic maintenance such as sealing or staining. For a no-maintenance option, consider Trex composite fencing.
Trex Seclusions is a composite privacy fencing system made from a blend of recycled wood and plastic. It won't rot, warp, or splinter and requires no painting or staining -- ideal for Hawaii's climate.
Bufftech CertaGrain is a premium vinyl fencing product with a realistic wood-grain texture. It gives you the natural look of wood with the durability and low maintenance of vinyl.
