Order Vinyl Fence Materials on Oahu

Materials We Carry

Upright Fencing Hawaii supplies vinyl fence materials across Oahu from our Kapolei shop. Order privacy and semi-privacy panels, vinyl posts and caps, rails, pickets, custom vinyl gates and gate hardware, in full sets or as individual parts.

Enter a quantity for each item you need, then send us your list. We will contact you to confirm availability and pricing.

We do not accept payments through this website. Please do not send money or financial information until our team has confirmed your order and given you payment instructions. ACH payments are made through a secure Intuit payment link sent by email, where you enter your own bank information. We do not require a voided check. Credit cards are processed through Clover. We do not accept wire transfers and will never ask you to email bank information. If you receive a payment request you are unsure about, call us at (808) 678-0331.

How to order

  • Choose your fence panels, then add posts. Every post comes with a cap. A straight run needs one more post than panels.
  • Only need a few parts? Most parts are also available on their own, depending on availability.
  • Photos show the style of each item. Choose your options and quantity under each one.

Fence Panels

Choose your picket, height, color and quantity. Panels can stretch to 8' wide with an aluminum insert in the bottom rail. Inserts are required for 3 1/2" rails, recommended for 6" rails, and sold separately. Fence heights under 5' are recommended at 6' on center (OC), or purchase aluminum inserts for the bottom rail. You can customize every material to your specific project. Posts are ordered separately below.

Privacy Panel

Includes rails, tongue & groove pickets and U-channels.

Semi-Privacy Panel

Our standard 6' H x 8' W panel has 6" pickets with 1-2" spacing. Includes rails and pickets.

This size needs an aluminum insert in the bottom rail. Inserts are sold separately under Rails & Aluminum Inserts.

We recommend a middle rail for 5' and 6' high fences with a 3 1/2" rail.
