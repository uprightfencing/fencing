Fencing for the Windward Side

Upright Fencing installs fences and gates island-wide, including Kailua, Kaneohe, and Waimanalo.

Windward Oahu gets more rain than the leeward side, along with steady trade winds and salt air near the coast. That makes low-maintenance vinyl and rust-free aluminum the most popular choices with our Windward customers. We also install chain link, redwood fencing, and custom gates for homes, HOAs, and commercial properties.
