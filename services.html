Our Fencing Services

What We Do

Comprehensive Fencing Solutions for Oahu

From residential privacy fences to large-scale commercial perimeter security, Upright Fencing Hawaii LLC delivers end-to-end fencing services. We handle everything in-house -- material sourcing, vinyl routing, custom gate fabrication, and professional installation -- so you get a single point of contact from estimate to completion.

Our Services

Fencing Solutions for Every Property

Vinyl Fencing

Our most popular fencing option comes with a lifetime manufacturer warranty on materials. Choose from privacy, semi-privacy, and picket styles in four colors: white, almond, tan, and gray. We route and fabricate vinyl in our Kapolei shop for faster turnaround and a precision fit on every project.

Learn More

Aluminum Fencing

Rust-free ornamental fencing that stands up to Hawaii's salt air without the maintenance of wrought iron. Ideal for pool enclosures (meets pool-safe code requirements), property boundaries, and decorative borders. Available in multiple heights and styles to complement any property.

Learn More

Chain Link Fencing

The most cost-effective fencing solution for residential and commercial properties. Built with galvanized or vinyl-coated frameworks for long-lasting durability. Add privacy slats in a range of colors for screening without sacrificing airflow. Great for yards, sports courts, and job sites.

Learn More

Wood & Composite

Natural beauty meets lasting performance. We install redwood and cedar fences for a classic look, plus Trex and Bufftech composite options that resist rot, warping, and insect damage without staining or sealing. Composite fencing is ideal for Hawaii's humid climate.

Learn More

Gates & Operators

Every gate is custom fabricated in our Kapolei shop to fit your exact opening. We build swing gates, sliding gates, and double gates in vinyl, aluminum, wood, and chain link. Add automated gate operators for convenience and security -- we install, program, and service all major brands.

Learn More

Commercial & Industrial

Security fencing, perimeter enclosures, and temporary construction fencing for commercial and industrial properties across Oahu. We work with general contractors, property managers, and government agencies to deliver compliant, durable fencing on schedule and on budget.
