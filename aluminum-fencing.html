Aluminum Fencing

The Look of Iron Without the Rust

Aluminum fencing is a rust-free, low-maintenance alternative to wrought iron. It's the ideal choice for Oahu properties that want the look of ornamental iron without the corrosion risk in our salt-air environment. Available in multiple styles and colors.

Benefits of Aluminum Fencing

01

Rust-Free

Aluminum won't corrode in Hawaii's salt air. Powder-coated finish resists UV damage.

02

Pool Safe

Meets pool safety code requirements. Popular for pool enclosures across Oahu.

03

Ornamental Style

The elegance of wrought iron without the maintenance. Multiple style options available.

Low Maintenance

No painting or rust treatment needed. Just occasional cleaning.
