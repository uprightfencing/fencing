About Upright Fencing Hawaii

Our Story

Family-Owned Since 1995

Upright Fencing Hawaii LLC was founded in 1995 right here in Kapolei. What started as a small, family-run operation has grown into one of Oahu's most trusted fence contractors -- built entirely on customer referrals and a commitment to doing things the right way.

For over 30 years, we have installed thousands of fences across Oahu, from residential privacy fencing in Ewa Beach to commercial security perimeters in Honolulu. Our growth has been steady and organic because we let our work speak for itself.

Based out of our Kapolei shop, we handle every step of the process in-house -- from vinyl routing and gate fabrication to final installation. That means fewer delays, tighter quality control, and a finished product we stand behind.

Our Capabilities

Everything is handled in-house at our Kapolei facility -- no subcontractors, no middlemen.

In-House Vinyl Routing

We fabricate and route vinyl fence components right in our Kapolei shop, cutting lead times and ensuring precision on every panel, post, and rail.

Custom Gate Fabrication

Swing gates, slide gates, and automated operators -- every gate is designed and welded to spec. No off-the-shelf compromises.

Full Welding Shop

Our on-site welding capabilities mean we can build custom brackets, frames, and structural components for any fencing or gate project.

Island-Wide Service

From the North Shore to Hawaii Kai, we serve every community on Oahu. Our crews know the terrain, the soil conditions, and the local building codes.
